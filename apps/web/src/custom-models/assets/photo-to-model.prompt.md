# Photo-to-model illustration

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the custom-model generation guide.
Stored as `photo-to-model.png`, resized to 960 × 640 for the interface.
The picture is illustrative, including the reference-photo panel.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a compact explanatory illustration for
Cabane's 'Generate a custom model' dialog. Primary request: show a real reference
photo becoming a personal 3D furniture model. Subject: on the left a slightly
tilted small printed photographic card showing a simple muted olive-green
upholstered armchair with natural wood legs in a neutral room; on the right the
same armchair recreated as an attractive simplified matte 3D model, with matching
shape and proportions. A subtle curved arrow connects photo to model. Style:
warm, restrained, polished 3D editorial illustration, soft natural shadows, cream
and olive tones matching a calm home design editor. Composition: wide horizontal,
two equally clear subjects, plenty of breathing room, centred, no cropping,
designed to remain legible at 480px wide. Background must be genuinely transparent
with clean alpha edges. Constraints: no words, no letters, no logos, no AI
branding, no UI mockup, no watermark, no extra decorative objects.
